Breakdown by client

Each property is one client and one invoice. The QuickBooks customer is the name on the meter at that address when it has one, otherwise the owner from the members list. Labor is each visit’s time on site, in the steps you pick (15 minutes, rounded up, unless you change it), × your rate; a visit with no time out has no labor. In QuickBooks: Settings ⚙ → Import data → Invoices. On the 1st of each month, last month’s invoices are made and emailed to you with a Review & send button on each. Nothing goes to a client until you approve it.

QuickBooks billing

In QuickBooks: Settings ⚙ → Import data → Invoices, upload one part at a time (QuickBooks takes 100 invoices per file), and tick “create customers that don't exist” only if you want new ones. Customers use the “QuickBooks customer” name on each meter when it has one. Product/service names must already exist in QuickBooks, or it uses “Sales”. Meters that weren't read, or read lower than last time, are left out.

Import the meter list

Upload the meter list: the .json file, or the Excel meter list (.xlsx), including after you've fixed names in it. Current Reading values in the Excel file are saved for this quarter. Meters that are already here keep their reading history. Meters are read once a quarter (Jan–Mar, Apr–Jun, Jul–Sep, Oct–Dec); each quarter's reading becomes the next quarter's “last”.

Google Drive backup

Every night at 2 AM, a copy of everything (hours, tasks, roads, water readings, meters, members) and any new photos goes to a Crew Hours Backups folder in your Google Drive. The app can only see the files it puts there.
